About Playful Bible Press

Playful Bible Press exists to produce beautiful books for children and adults together to explore the Bible.

Fun to read, fun to look at, and faithful to the Bible text.

Our books are fun to read, and at the same time we take care to stick closely to the Bible text. We try not to oversimplify or add our own interpretation. If the Bible raises questions to encourage prayer and meditation, we want to do the same. A Playful Bible book should spark curiosity, starting conversations and sending you to the Bible.

100% human written and illustrated! At Playful Bible Press we think AI can be a useful tool for research or making reference images, but we never put AI generations inside our books. All poems and artwork are 100% human created.
