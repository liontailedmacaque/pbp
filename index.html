A Curious Letter
from Nebuchadnezzar

A king. A strange dream. And a lesson he would never forget.

King Nebuchadnezzar ruled the mighty city of Babylon. But one night, a terrifying dream left him searching for answers. Only Daniel could tell him what it meant.

Inspired by the extraordinary letter found in Daniel chapter 4, A Curious Letter from Nebuchadnezzar retells the biblical story of a proud king brought low — and the God whose kingdom lasts for ever.

Told in rhyme and brought to life with beautiful watercolour illustrations, this is a Bible story for children and grown-ups to discover together.
