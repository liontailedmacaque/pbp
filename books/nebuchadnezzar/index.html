A Curious Letter
from Nebuchadnezzar

A king. A strange dream. And a lesson he would never forget.

King Nebuchadnezzar ruled the mighty city of Babylon. But one night, a dream left him searching for answers.

Only Daniel could tell him what it meant.

Inspired by the extraordinary letter found in Daniel chapter 4, from the Bible, A Curious Letter from Nebuchadnezzar retells the story of a king who learns a lesson in pride.

Told in rhyme, closely following the Bible text, and brought to life with beautiful watercolour illustrations, this is a Bible story for children and grown-ups to discover together.

About the book

A Curious Letter from Nebuchadnezzar is a rhyming retelling of Daniel chapter 4.

King Nebuchadnezzar has a strange dream. Daniel gives him an honest warning, but God teaches him through experience.

Written and illustrated by John Holden.

Published by Playful Bible Press, an imprint of JH Creative Studio Ltd.

ISBN: 978-1-0654498-0-5

The author says:

“Children will have fun seeing King Nebuchadnezzar in bed. I haven't tried to avoid anachronisms — but Babylon experts will enjoy the architecture and wall art, not to mention King Neb's crown which is based on Babylonian sculpture at the British Museum. Speaking of which, watch the crown for a story within the story....”
