AI Policy

The text in our books is written by people, and all our illustrations are drawn by people. At Playful Bible Press, we think AI can be a useful tool, but we want everything our children read and see in our books to be drawn and written by people, not machines.

We never use AI in the following ways:

❌ AI-generated writing, even in early drafts. For example, "ChatGPT, write a rhyme about the Transfiguration." We never use AI to generate text for the inside of our books.

❌ AI-generated artwork in our books. All final illustrations, including cover artwork, are drawn by people.

We use AI as a tool in the following ways:

✅ As a starting point for research. For example, "ChatGPT, which Old Testament passage is referenced by 'the Chosen One' in Luke's account of the Transfiguration?" AI can help us identify where to look, but we never rely on AI for our theology. Suggestions are always followed up through reliable commentaries, sermons and personal Bible study.

✅ To generate reference images. For example, "ChatGPT, generate five images of a Babylonian servant bowing to the king." We sometimes use these images as references for our illustrators. However, the final illustrations that appear in our books are always drawn, coloured and painted by people, never generated by AI. We may also make ordinary digital adjustments to the finished artwork, such as colour contrast.

✅ To assist with promotional writing. We sometimes use AI to help draft or improve promotional text, including website content and book blurbs. People remain responsible for writing, editing and approving the final material. AI-generated text is never used inside our books.
