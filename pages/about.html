About Playful Bible Press

Playful Bible Press exists to produce beautiful books for children and adults to explore the Bible together.

Fun to read, fun to look at, and faithful to the Bible text.

Our vision is for our children's bookshelves to be filled with engaging books of Bible stories, from the classic favourites to the more obscure.

We take care to follow the Bible text closely, without oversimplifying or adding unnecessary interpretation. Where the Bible leaves room for questions and reflection, we want our books to do the same.

Written and illustrated by people. Read our AI Policy.
